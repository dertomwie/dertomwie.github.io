In this wiki some of the most important general knowledge about the realm of Prima Orbis can be found. A lot of this information is intended as a reminder for players, but some information may be hidden to avoid potential spoilers.

Pantheon

Due to their fondness of messing around in the planes of existence the gods and their respective worshipers have a large influence on the beliefs and values of most residents of the planes and on societies as a whole. Thus, understanding the pantheon of Prima Orbis can help estimating how NPCs will react to certain proposals or actions.
