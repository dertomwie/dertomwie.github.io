Pantheon of Prima Orbis

The pantheon of Prima Orbis is a collection of ten gods. Gods, in this world, are supernatural beings that existed "before" (time doesn't make much sense in this context) the planes of existence and cannot cease to exist. They are essentially almighty and their control over the planes of existence is complete. However, they cannot (and do not) claim to be truely almighty, since they are obviously not: Among other things, they can neither destroy themselves nor each other and they cannot create new true gods like themselves. Though they sometimes create mortal avatars of themselves or god-like creatures with increased control over one or multiple planes, if they deem it practical or entertaining, leading some mortals to worship these as their deities as well.
