Pax (god)

Pax is the god of peace, conflict, and bravery in the pantheon of Prima Orbis. Of all deities, he is most commonly worshipped for protection. Many connect a feeling of valor or courage with him. He doesn't intervene often, but when he does, he changes the course of history.

Worshipers

Pax is most commonly worshiped by soldiers, generals and politicians in times of conflict. They pray for a quick end to the given conflict with little suffering or, if they are desperate, for protection. He is worshiped across the planes of existence by those defending their homeland, be it a fiend or celestial, mortal or immortal.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

Most soldiers carry tiny metal shields with them, which they clean like their actual weapons and armor. Before fights they hold these close to their heart or mouth and send prayers to Pax. After combat, when the wounded are being treated and silence starts to settle in, they start their cleaning rituals.

Temples of Pax can be found in nearly every major city (both in the material and other planes) and in many smaller settlements in border regions that have seen war or expect to see war some day. These temples are usually made of solid rock, allowing them to be repurposed as a shelter for civilians during an attack. The priests there like to remind those with an open ear of the horrors war brings with it and hold regular sermons to remind anyone who listens of the wars of the past.

Stories

Many stories about interventions of Pax are told by the priests in his temples. One of the most common stories deals with the final battle of the last major war between Agornum and Orsana. This battle is widely regarded as a turning point in history, marking both the start of more stable diplomacy between the empires and increased interventions by the more appreciated gods who had left the mortals on their own after the age of darkness.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Pax that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

Pax watches the actions of the other gods with great concern. He was the one to suggest not intervening with the material plane anymore when the age of darkness began. Though it became obvious that some deities broke their agreement more often than others and thus, Pax decided to break the agreement himself, which unfortunately made the other deities ignore it with even lass care.

Though for mortals it may seem like Pax spends much of his attention on the material plane, keeping the war between demons and devils within their realms is his ultimate goal since their forces might be capable of ending mortal civilizations forever.
