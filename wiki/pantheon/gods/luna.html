Luna (goddess)

Luna is the goddess of balance, nature, and justice in the pantheon of Prima Orbis. She is perceived as the deity caring most about "lesser" lifeforms like small elementals, animals, and minor spirits. The feeling that is most commonly attributed to her is contemptness. Her interventions are focussed on restoring natural order and are rarely witnessed by more than a few sapient beings.

Worshipers

Most druids worship Luna and try their best to live in harmony with nature. Furthermore, judges pray to Luna for an unwavering sense of justice. Outside of the Material Plane, Luna is worshipped by celestials, greater elementals, and devils, al of whom seek order in their societies. Lastly, the inhabitants of the Court see themselves as Luna's creations, but they do not actively worship her in order to remain unbiased.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

It is widely believed that one can obtain the favor of Luna by tending to a pet or wild animals. Most worshipers of Luna choose carnivorous pets like cats, hawks, or owls for their more active role in the natural order.

For worshippers of Luna on the material plane, the moon is a common symbol because it represents the eternal balance between darkness and light, as it cycles from new moon to full moon and back again. It is used to mark most entrances to courts in major cities and to adorn many shrines in remote locations. These places are often visited by druids, who lend an ear to those seeking to restore balance in their lives or resolve internal conflicts. These druids are viewed somewhat like priests, and if enough people gather at a shrine or court, they may deliver a speech or perform a ritual such as conjuring small animals or communicating with plants, to encourage listeners to lead a life more in harmony with nature.

Stories

Tales of animals emanating moonlight and guiding sapients to rectify imbalances have existed since before the Age of Darkness. One of the most widespread tales concerns a judge who was assigned a complex political assassination case. Just before he was about to rule the defendant guilty, a shimmering owl seemingly appeared out of nowhere and presented a piece of evidence that had been overlooked. Ultimately, the defendant was found not guilty, and the actual assassin was identified.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Luna that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

In Luna's view, there is a strict hierarchy of lifeforms. The least valuable are the immortals, who alter the planes at will and destroy order without noticing. Next are the sapient mortals, who are forced to live with the surrounding nature, but try to change it to their advantage wherever possible. The most valuable are non-sapient lifeforms. Animals that never try to change the world around them, elementals that follow the flow of the elements, and spirits that guard sacred places or haunt unholy ground are the foundation of the planes in her view and thus require care and affection.

Although Sol appreciates Luna because of her efforts to spread justice, Luna does not feel the same way about Sol. To her, Sol's efforts to create and restore life are a constant offence against the natural order.
