Umbrax (deity)

Umbrax is the deity of mystery and wonder in the pantheon of Prima Orbis. No mortal claims to understand its intentions or its reasoning. Many connect it to confusion, some to curiosity. Its interventions are sporadic and without obvious cause.

Worshipers

Umbrax is not worshiped like the other gods. Since he doesn't seem to care about worship anyways, he draws the attention and favor of those who want to go their own way in life. Some writers and librarians and many explorers that seek the wonders of the unknown feel a special connection to Umbrax. Some refer to themselves as observed ones. This not only applies for mortals, but also for many aberrations and some major fiends.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

Many worshipers of Umbrax write notes or diaries in old languages, documenting their environments and their experiences within. Additionally, some keep pets of species that are perceived as Umbrax' creations like cats or ravens. Other than that, they don't actively pray or provide offerings.

Counterintuitively, most cities and even many small towns house temples of Umbrax. These are usually dark, silent places that have an unnaturally calming atmosphere. Cats seem to be unusually attracted to them and it is common to see them sitting on the inside of the darkened windows. For most of these temples, it is unknown who created or maintains them, but even in abandoned cities they often remain as the most intact buildings for a long while. Although you rarely see someone enter a temple of Umbrax, everyone meets someone preparing food or water for cats inside the temple. These figures are generally regarded as priests of Umbrax although they don't claim this themselves. After the first visit, one generally finds the temple empty unless company is explicitly appreciated in times of sorrow or hardship.

Stories

It is told that sometimes, when nobody watches, a dimly gleaming eye appears on the should of someone reading or writing an interesting story. As soon as the eye is witnessed, it vanishes. But a feeling of being watched remains until the night, when unusual dreams come to the reader or writer. These dreams are described as experiences that cannot be put into words, but leave the witness in awe for some days.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Umbrax that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

Umbrax created the first conscious being, Yule Anayechunguza, also known as "the one who watches". It had a major impact on the development of life in the young material plane it was cast into. More about it will be added in a different entry.

Umbrax has a native form, but it is usually obscured by an opaque coating, usually imitating an eye that is filled with a galaxy, black hole, nebula, or some similar astral phenomenon. Only Ratia knows his true form. It was one of her major fixations before she created the material plane. Mortals are not able to comprehend its true form.
