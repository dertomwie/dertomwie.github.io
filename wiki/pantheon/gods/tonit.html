Tonit (god)

Tonit is the god of change and discord in the pantheon of Prima Orbis. He is the god most commonly attributed with influence over the elements. Many connect him to the feeling of anger. He does not shy away from interventions, conjuring blistering storms to support rebellions, awaking volcanoes in times of stability, or crushing tsunamis against the shores of countries that outlived his goodwill.

Worshipers

Tonit is most commonly worshiped by rebels and outcasts. They rarely form large organisations, but in rare cases worshipers of Tonit become a serious problem for unsuspecting dictators or instable republics. Other than those occasions he is considered a minor deity with little impact on society on the material plane. In contrast, on the elemental planes he is by far the most worshiped god.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

The most effective ritual for worshipers of Tonit has shown to be public protests in the streets of major cities. Though, where that wasn't feasible due to restrictive laws or guard brutality, causing chaos from the underground also resulted in responses from Tonit.

On the material plane temples for Tonit are rare. Some of his worshipers create their own personal shrines in abandoned buildings or hideouts, but most, especially those rejected by their society, try to leave no evidence of their prayers. They rather keep some religious symbol by themselves, which they often hide underneath outer layers of clothing. These symbols are often made from simple materials like old iron or rugged wood and symbolize storms, volcano eruptions or other natural disasters.

Stories

Rumor has it that Tonit was the god who started sending the world into the age of darkness and convinced the others to follow his example.

Since then, he is made responsible for enabling at least two regime changes in Orsana, of which one was executed in a stormy night, during which multiple guards were reportedly struck by lightning, and one took place during an earthquake that buried the emperor at the time under tons of stone, when a rupture opened up under his palace and took the whole building with it. Both of these events reportedly took place after months of public uprising.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Tonit that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

The first story about Tonit is not entirely true, since he didn't actively decide to send the world into the age of darkness. The exact reason for that going the way it did, are revealed in the diary of Prof. Eliana Fidelis (, which may not yet be available in this wiki).

Tonit created the elemental planes and their inhabitants after realizing that he enjoyed their nature. His greatest creation, however, is the fifth element, which he embedded into the material plane by himself. Although she doesn't like to admit it, even Ratia was surprised by the perfection of this creation, especially coming from someone so uninterested in order.
