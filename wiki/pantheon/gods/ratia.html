Ratia (goddess)

Ratia is the goddess of logic, reason and by extension science. Of all deities she is most commonly believed to be omniscient. Despite, or maybe because of, this, she rarely intervenes in the planes of existence, acting as a silent observer.

Worshipers

Ratia is most commonly worshiped my those in the pursuit of knowledge over the world. Many scientists, engineers and wizards pray for a flash of brilliance or an answer to some riddle in their studies, though it is widely accepted that she rarely, if ever, listens to such requests. Besides the material plane, where she is seen as one of the major deities and almost every city has a place of worship for her, she is mostly considered as a minor deity, being worshiped only be excentric beings in hidden libraries.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

Due to a lack of responses to attempted rituals, none ultimately stuck and most worshipers have their own little rituals that they go through every day like spending some minutes on memorizing digits of Π or solving some small riddle.

The temples of Ratia are usually close to universities in cities that have universities. They are used as places for gatherings between scientists, scholars or students to discuss recent advancements in their fields, learn together or just generally hang out. Usually, there are no dedicated priests to these temples, but they are upheld by some of their most influential visitors. Sermons are very rare and are generally held as special events when scientists from different places come together for a conference or some similar gathering.

Stories

It is widely believed that Ratia pays a visit to some of her most influential worshipers in the moments before their death. As the stories go, she only ever visits them, when they are alone. She then asks them to drink a cup of tee with her and speak about their findings one last time. Everyone reporting of such a meeting describes it as very pleasant. Ratia is found to be a patient listener, taking notes on everything being said and asking quesitons whenever something is unclear. Her corporal appearance is reported to be of humanoid shape with a silvery skin, engraved with fractals. She is said to wear clothes appropriate to scientists of the respective time, have a warm voice and a look in her eyes that implies interest in what is said.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Ratia that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

Ratia created the material plane as the first self-sustaining plane. Before, no god had ever created a potentially permanent plane with coherent physics and afterwards, the other planes were created using the material plane as a blueprint. During the creation of the material plane, she asked for the assistance of Umbrax in order to add more mystery to the world.

Of all the gods, Umbrax is the one Ratia would be most likely to call a 'friend', although the concept of friendship is quite foreign to the deities. They are complementary and find great joy in exploring the nature of each other.
