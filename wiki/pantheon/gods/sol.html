Sol (deity)

Sol is the deity of life and energy. Of all deities it is most commonly attributed with pure goodness. She is quick to cause a minor intervention, sending one of her angels to rectify some evil or help someone in need.

Worshipers

Sol is worshiped by the majority of mortal creatures, but sentient plants and those who grow plants take the greatest effort to appease her. They often pray for good weather, good harvests or general goodwill. In the material plane every city and most towns have cathedrals or temples for Sol and many farmers have personal shrines in their homes. Outside of the material plane Sol is worshiped by most good-aligned creatures in their own respective ways.

Rituals, Signs and Temples

The largest ritual of worship for Sol takes place around summer solstice and is celebrated across the material plane. During the festivities, people plant seeds or saplings and promise to nurture them and bring them through the winter. In addition they pray for a mild winter and good harvests until then.

Temples of Sol are decorated with symbols of the sun and decorative plants that are cared for by dedicated priests. These priests are oftentimes proficient in healing magic and many work as midwifes when they are needed. Many of these temples have their own gardens and large windows, making them bright and calming places. The priests of Sol try to talk to anyone entering the temples to lend them open ears or lighten their day with some fable. They don't hold large speeches or gatherings, but trust in those who seek help or refuge to find their ways to the temples naturally.

Stories

In some cities in Agornum groves remained nearly unharmed during the age of darkness, granting people refuge from the chaotic world around them. These groves are assumed to be protected by Sol and continue to prosper to this day through every cold winter and dry summer.

Tales of demonic and devilish invasion forces most commonly end with angels descending from the sky or erupting from portals leading directly to the surface of the sun and slaying the fiendish invaders.

Major Spoilers

Background information on Sol that is not generally available to anyone in the realm of Prima Orbis and may contain major spoilers.

Sol is quite naive. It does not really understand how one being could enjoy the suffering of another. However, it is also hard for Sol to use the right amount of energy on something. In the early days of the material plane, Sol accidentaly burnt entire planets, trying to kickstart the creation of life. To this day, it mostly refrains from interacting with the world by itself and usually sends the angels of its own plane whenever it feels, it should intervene. Sol's garden is a plane where energy is abundant and energy conservation laws are suspended. The angels born in this plane are armed with a strong sence of justice and usually act as servants to Sol, Ratia, Luna and, sometimes, Splendor. There are also occasionally angels that work on their own behalf or follow the orders of other gods.

Sol spends much time in discussions with Luna, since Sol's favor for abundance is quite contradictory to Luna's beloved balance. Other than that, Sol enjoys speaking to all the other gods and, on occasion, beings from the realms of Prima Orbis to learn new perspectives, find ways to help the world or just generally have a good time.
