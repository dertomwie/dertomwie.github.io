Fey Maze

This riddle resembles a shifting maze that changes while adventures travel through it. The paths between fields are (in contrary to the visual representation) winding and from the middle of one tile to the next takes average adventurers about 3 minutes.

Maze






Hints

Tiles react when you leave them and turn accordingly.


Tiles always rotate clockwise to the next available exit.


Did you try turning around more?

Advice for running this riddle

For this riddle players should have access to aerial surveillance like a flying familiar or movable sensor.

Do not reveal the visual indicator of the maze immediatly, but wait for them to figure out that something is wrong by themselves. Until then, describe how they navigate the winding paths and maybe, if they do not figure it out, describe that some tile they previously visited appears familiar to them.

If they want to leave marks, take notes. This is one of the easiest ways for the players to figure out what is going on.

Ideally you might also add some fey that inhabit certain areas of the maze and talk to travellers, potentially misguiding or helping them.
